Bird Sightings around USC

This interactive web map shows bird observations from iNaturalist around USC and nearby neighborhoods. Users can filter observations by species and date and click on the map to view more information about individual observations.

Explore the Map

Use the filters to explore bird observations by species and date. Click on a point to view details about an individual observation.

Source: iNaturalist observation data. Bird observations were selected for the USC area and nearby neighborhoods.

Note: Each point represents an observation recorded on iNaturalist and does not represent the complete distribution of birds in the area.
